<p>OI Shopping List makes it easy to create and manage shopping lists and other checklists. Add items to a list, check them off in the shop, and clean up the list when you are done.</p>

<p>Free, open source and ad-free since 2009. The app needs no internet permission: your lists stay on your device.</p>

<p><b>Features</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Several lists, e.g. groceries, hardware store, wish list</li>
<li>Add items quickly, with suggestions from items you used before</li>
<li>Check items off with undo; clean up bought items with one tap</li>
<li>Pick mode: re-add items you bought before</li>
<li>Quantity, units, priority, tags and notes for each item</li>
<li>Prices per store, with totals for the list</li>
<li>Sort order, hide checked items, font size and themes</li>
<li>Home screen widget</li>
<li>Import and export lists as CSV (OI Shopping List and HandyShopper formats)</li>
<li>Share a list as text, or add items shared from other apps</li>
<li>Automation (e.g. Tasker) to clean up lists</li>
<li>Available in about 40 languages</li>
</ul>

<p>Source code and issue tracker: https://github.com/openintents/shoppinglist</p>
